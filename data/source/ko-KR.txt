## hsk1-16
what | 뭐 | | 이게 뭐예요? | What is this? | 무엇 |
how | 어떻게 | | 이 글자는 어떻게 써요? | How do you write this letter? | |
how-is-it | 어때요 | | 일은 어때요? | How is your job? | 어떻습니까 |
- ma | Korean asks yes/no questions with the verb ending, not a separate word.
- ne | Korean adds 는요? to a word instead (저는요? "and me?").
de | 의 | | ^민수의 책이에요. | It's Minsu's book. | | In speech, 저의 is usually shortened to 제 (my).
- le | Korean marks finished actions with a verb ending (-었-), not a separate word.
- not-past | Korean puts 안 (not) before the verb; 안 here is "inside", and negation comes in a later level.
+topic-marker | 는 | (topic marker: as for...) | 저는 학생이에요. | I am a student. | 은 | After a vowel 는, after a consonant 은.
+subject-marker | 가 | (subject marker) | 고양이가 귀여워요. | The cat is cute. | 이 | After a vowel 가, after a consonant 이.
+object-marker | 를 | (object marker) | 저는 사과를 먹어요. | I eat an apple. | 을 | After a vowel 를, after a consonant 을.
+place-of-action | 에서 | at, in (where something happens) | 저는 학교에서 공부해요. | I study at school. | |
